# Pulse Generation UG

**INNOVATE. BUILD. TRANSFORM.**

Pulse Generation UG is a Kampala software company building offline-first apps for real African conditions: systems that keep working when the internet and power do not, keep data on your own computers, and sync branches when a connection is available.

## Our apps

| App | For |
|---|---|
| [EduPulse](https://pulsegenerationug.com/edupulse/) | Schools, colleges and universities |
| [PulseHMIS](https://pulsegenerationug.com/pulsehmis/) | Hospitals and clinics |
| [PulseLMIS](https://pulsegenerationug.com/pulselmis/) | Medical laboratories |
| [PulseRemit Pro](https://pulsegenerationug.com/pulseremit-pro/) | Banks, SACCOs and money transfer |
| [PulseProperty Pro](https://pulsegenerationug.com/pulseproperty/) | Rentals and property management |
| [PulseStock](https://pulsegenerationug.com/pulsestock/) | Shops, pharmacies and stock |
| [MotoPulse](https://pulsegenerationug.com/motopulse/) | Car wash, parking and garages |
| [Lwasa Ludo](https://pulsegenerationug.com/lwasa-ludo/) | Free 3D Ludo with Luganda commentary |

Windows, Android and Mac. Free install, 30-day free trial, staff training included.
